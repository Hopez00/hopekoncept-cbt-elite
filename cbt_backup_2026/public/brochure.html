JAMB UTME 2026 Guidelines & Subject Combinations for All Careers

The Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board requires every candidate to choose 4 subjects corresponding to their chosen course of study across Engineering, Medicine, Sciences, Law, Arts, Social Sciences, and Commercial disciplines.

Engineering & Technology

  • Compulsory: Use of English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry.
  • Target Careers: Civil Engineering, Mechanical, Electrical, Computer Engineering, Architecture.

Medical & Health Sciences

  • Compulsory: Use of English, Biology, Chemistry, Physics or Mathematics.
  • Target Careers: Medicine & Surgery, Pharmacy, Nursing Science, Medical Laboratory Science.

Arts, Law & Humanities

  • Compulsory: Use of English, Literature-in-English, Government, CRS or History.
  • Target Careers: Law, Mass Communication, Linguistics, International Relations, Theatre Arts.

Commercial & Social Sciences

  • Compulsory: Use of English, Mathematics, Economics, and either Commerce, Accounting or Government.
  • Target Careers: Accounting, Banking & Finance, Business Administration, Economics, Marketing.

WAEC Senior School Certificate Examination (SSCE) Complete Syllabi

Comprehensive West African syllabus covering all senior secondary school subjects tested in objective (Paper 1), theory (Paper 2), and practicals (Paper 3).

Mathematics Syllabus

  • Number and numeration, algebraic processes, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, statistics, and probability.

English Language Syllabus

  • Comprehension, summary, vocabulary, grammar (lexis and structure), continuous writing, and oral English.

Physics Syllabus

  • Mechanics, thermal physics, waves, optics, electricity, magnetism, atomic and nuclear physics.

Chemistry Syllabus

  • Matter, atomic structure, periodic table, chemical bonding, stoichiometry, acids/bases/salts, organic chemistry.

Biology Syllabus

  • Living organisms, cell structure, plant and animal physiology, ecology, heredity, variation, and evolution.

Economics Syllabus

  • Basic economic concepts, consumer behavior, theory of demand and supply, market structures, national income, public finance, international trade.
