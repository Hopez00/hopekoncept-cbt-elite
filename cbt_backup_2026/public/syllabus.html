Comprehensive 2026 UTME Subject Syllabus

Review recommended examination topics, core objectives, and guidelines set by JAMB for all subjects.

Use of English

  • Comprehension & Summary: Reading for implied meaning, critical analysis, and vocabulary in context.
  • Lexis & Structure: Synonyms, antonyms, idioms, clause structures, agreement, and grammatical precision.
  • Oral Forms: Vowels, consonants, rhymes, word stress, and intonation patterns.

Mathematics

  • Number & Numeration: Indices, logarithms, standard form, sets, and sequences.
  • Algebra: Polynomials, quadratic equations, inequalities, matrices, and determinants.
  • Geometry & Trigonometry: Euclidean geometry, mensuration, bearings, and trigonometric ratios/identities.
  • Calculus & Statistics: Differentiation, integration, measures of dispersion, and probability.

Physics

  • Interactions of Matter: Kinematics, dynamics, work, energy, power, elasticity, and fluids.
  • Waves: Sound waves, light waves, electromagnetic spectrum, and optical instruments.
  • Thermodynamics & Fields: Heat transfer, electrostatics, current electricity, magnetism, and electromagnetic induction.

Chemistry

  • Physical Chemistry: Stoichiometry, atomic structure, states of matter, energetic, and chemical equilibria.
  • Inorganic Chemistry: Periodic table properties, metals, non-metals, and qualitative analysis.
  • Organic Chemistry: Functional groups, hydrocarbons, alkanols, alkanoates, polymers, and biochemistry.

Biology

  • Variety of Organisms & Cell Biology: Cell structure, organization, movement, and biological molecules.
  • Plant & Animal Physiology: Nutrition, respiration, transport systems, excretion, and regulation.
  • Genetics & Ecology: Heredity, variation, evolution, trophic levels, nutrient cycles, and environmental pollution.

Economics

  • Microeconomics: Utility theory, consumer behavior, market structures, price determination, and demand/supply dynamics.
  • Macroeconomics: National income accounting, inflation, unemployment, public finance, monetary policy, and international trade.

Government

  • Political Concepts & Systems: Power, authority, legitimacy, democracy, monarchy, constitutions, and federalism.
  • Nigerian Government History: Pre-colonial systems, constitutional developments, military rule, public service, and foreign policy.

Literature-in-English

  • Drama & Prose: African and non-African plays/novels, plot development, characterization, themes, and narrative techniques.
  • Poetry & Literary Devices: Figures of speech, rhyme schemes, tone, mood, and analysis of unseen poems.

Christian Religious Studies (CRS)

  • Themes from Old Testament: Creation, leadership of Moses, Joshua, the monarchy, and prophetic messages.
  • Themes from New Testament: Birth and ministry of Jesus Christ, passion, resurrection, and the early apostolic church.
